Common Questions

Q1

What software do you use for coloring?

I primarily use Adobe Photoshop combined with a Wacom Cintiq for maximum precision. For certain stylized projects, I also utilize Clip Studio Paint for its unique brush engine and atmospheric lighting tools.

Q2

How long does it take to color a single issue?

A standard 22-page comic issue typically takes between 2 to 3 weeks. This timeframe accounts for initial palette development, flatting, rendering, and final color correction passes to ensure consistency.

Q3

Do you provide high-resolution files for print?

Absolutely. All final deliveries include print-ready CMYK files at 300-600 DPI, along with optimized RGB versions for digital platforms, Comixology, and social media promotion.
