STYLE GUIDE

My artistic signature, palettes, and process.

1. Color Philosophy

My approach to coloring is narrative first. Color should not just be realistic; it must serve the story. I use highly saturated pops of color against restricted, muted backgrounds to draw the reader's eye precisely where it needs to go.

Light sources are exaggerated, shadows are deep, and I frequently utilize halftone textures to pay homage to the rich history of printed comic books.

2. Genre Palettes

Horror

Desaturated cool grays, deep blacks, and aggressive blood red accents.

Superhero

Bold primary colors, high contrast, and dynamic lighting effects.

Sci-Fi

Neon accents, synthetic magenta/cyan splits, and cool steel grays.

3. Process Walkthrough

Phase 1: Flatting

Establishing local colors and creating precise selection masks for every element on the page.

Phase 2: Shading & FX

Adding volume through cel-shading or soft rendering, establishing lighting, and applying atmospheric FX.

4. Tools & Software

Photoshop CC

Industry standard for comic coloring and final CMYK prep.

Clip Studio Paint

Excellent for flatting and precise lineart adjustments.

Cintiq Pro 24

Large canvas workspace for detailed rendering.

5. Typography & Lettering

While I don't letter the books, my color choices always respect the balloon placement. I ensure high contrast behind text areas so the dialogue remains perfectly legible. A colorist's job is to enhance the reading experience, not distract from it.
