Every page has a pulse. My job is to find it. I'm a professional comic book colorist specializing in bold, narrative-driven palettes that elevate the lineart and guide the reader's eye.

From atmospheric horror to vibrant superhero splashes, I work closely with pencillers and editors to ensure the final product pops off the page.
